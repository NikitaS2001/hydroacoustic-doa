## Project Overview

- **Question:** Can phase-aware JEPA/JEPA-like channel and array representation learning improve single-source underwater DOA azimuth estimation over matched scratch and classical methods on a **measured linear array**, with controlled simulation and obligatory under-ice recordings in winter 2026–2027? A known identifiable sector and constrained source elevation are needed; no unambiguous 360° or physical arbitrary-topology claim.
- **Target artifact:** complete Candidate of Sciences dissertation text by **2027-03-31**, plus a main submission-ready manuscript package and reproducible method/evaluation artifacts. Publication requirements and target journal/venue are **unconfirmed**; submission is not acceptance. Scope and milestones: [`roadmap.md`](docs/research/framework/roadmap.md#27-minimum-deliverables-and-additional-studies).
- **Data:** controlled simulated independent environments, with receiver-specific saved direct component `D_m`; future independently acquired, labelled winter linear-array sessions; separately authorized unlabelled real-development group(s) only for track R; distinct labelled development adaptation E1; sealed final real groups excluded from every fit. There is **no accepted project field dataset yet**. See [`data_and_simulation.md`](docs/research/framework/data_and_simulation.md), [`winter_field_protocol.md`](docs/experiments/winter_field_protocol.md).

## AI Research Context

- **Core hypothesis, not result:** Stage 1 single-channel JEPA A/S (`X_m` → EMA latent of simulated receiver direct `D_m`, retaining delay/phase); a **frozen E relative-phase/TDOA probe gate before Stage 2**; Stage 2 trains Fusion with E frozen to predict pre-pooling full-array EMA latent tokens, random hidden channel count/identities (`N_good≥3`, ≥2 visible, ≥1 hidden), loss **only hidden**; Stage 3 has **parallel** frozen E+Fusion/new-head and jointly fine-tuned E+Fusion/other-new-head descendants of the same SSL checkpoint. B/R uses authorized unlabelled observed future windows, B/S is a matched data-source control; real A/R needs an independently validated estimated direct `D_hat`. Exact A/S–B/R composition, mask law, budget, size and formal M3 scope remain open. Canonical [`training §13.5`](docs/research/framework/training_strategy.md#135-three-stage-jepa-protocol-and-gating).
- **Closest precedents:** [I-JEPA (Assran et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08243) for latent EMA prediction; related multisensor methods and their non-transferability discussed in [`multisensor review`](outputs/multisensor_jepa_literature_review.md). Underwater channel/path limitations in [`direct-arrival review`](outputs/direct_path_real_jepa_literature_review.md). None establishes project DOA performance.
- **Required baselines/ablations:** matched supervised-from-scratch coordinate-aware and no-coordinate pair, MVDR/Capon and MUSIC (Bartlett diagnostic); when feasible, Stage-1-only versus Stage-1+2 and matched downstream budgets. Do **not** resurrect the VAE/HuBERT five-encoder E4 matrix. Full specification: [`evaluation.md`](docs/research/framework/evaluation.md).
- **Primary measures:** supervised loss is MSE on **raw** `(a,b)` toward `(cos θ,sin θ)`; independent-group sector-aware circular azimuth error (median/p95), invalid-vector and source/QA failure rate, coverage, uncertainty and resource costs reported separately. Frozen-E gate: paired signed TDOA and relative circular phase versus raw/untrained E with independent simulated reporting scenes; thresholds **TODO: development/physical error budget**, not a claimed pass. [`evaluation §18`](docs/research/framework/evaluation.md#18-evaluation-metrics-and-evidence).
- **Measurement pilot, not fixed signal:** user-reported initial ULA-8, approximately 27-mm pitch, 96 ksample/s, stated «32 bit» (effective ADC format and underwater spacing unknown). Proposed *bench candidate* LFM 4–20 kHz, 50 ms, Re/Im STFT 256/64; source and usable received band **unverified**. [`signal review`](outputs/signal_waveform_frontend_review.md); calculation input/code and stdout in [`experiments/`](experiments/).

## Ground Rules and Data Access

- Never modify raw data in `Data/Raw/` or equivalent; keep raw archives immutable with checksums, calibration, group/access ledgers and separately sealed final groups. No closed-test labels, noise, unlabelled clips or derived data may fit an encoder/EMA, normalizer, simulator or selector.
- Read first, act second; keep one canonical written outcome per investigation under `outputs/` (paper drafts in `papers/`), plans/logs in `notes/`, executable code and measured outputs in `experiments/`. Include direct primary-source URLs and trace quantitative claims to a source or raw local calculation. Preserve S sim-only, R authorized unlabelled, E1 labelled adaptation and sealed evaluation as distinct.
- Winter field safety/permissions and real underwater truth take priority over optional experiments. The main three-stage JEPA direction is adopted, but **whether completing all stages is a formal minimum remains subject to candidate/supervisor sign-off**; document a blocked/failed gate rather than claiming completion.
